# ShareLink privacy

ShareLink collects no data.

Passwords and usernames used to sign in stay in the device Keychain. They are not written to logs, diagnostics, UserDefaults, or the metadata database. Diagnostics redact usernames and server host names.

Connections go only to the SMB servers you add or that your organization configures with managed app configuration. ShareLink does not use analytics and does not include a third-party SDK that collects data.

Questions and reports go through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ajthom90/ShareLink/issues).
